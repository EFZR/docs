# Personal Task List

This document helps me build better habits by tracking the key activities I want to focus on in my daily life.

## Core Tasks

These are the main responsibilities and habits I want to maintain regularly:

* **Clean the house**
  Keep my living space tidy and organized. *(Aim: daily or weekly)*

* **Help my brother with his homework**
  Support him with his studies and stay involved. *(As needed)*

* **Work on my job**
  Stay focused during working hours and maintain productivity.

* **Learn my Go course**
  Dedicate regular time to study and practice Go programming. *(Set specific days/times)*

* **Go to the gym**
  Maintain a consistent workout routine for health and fitness.

* **Spend time with my girlfriend**
  Dedicate at least one day per week to be fully present with her. *(More if possible)*

## Bad Habits

These are habits that prevent me from staying consistent with my goals:

* **Sleeping late at night**
  Leads to tiredness and reduced productivity the next day.

* **Not waking up early**
  Makes it harder to start the day with energy and structure.

* **Doing tasks at the wrong time**
  Poor time management reduces effectiveness and breaks focus.

* **Avoiding tasks for unjustified reasons**
  Procrastinating or making excuses slows down progress and builds inconsistency.
